    For control engineers: transfer function, margins, Bode, Nyquist, root locus

    Bode and Nyquist: continuous vehicle behind a zero-order hold (delay T/2 plus latency), with the 20 Hz controller and actuator elements evaluated exactly at z = e^(sT). Poles and the predicted response: the exact 20 Hz discrete loop. Amber points and dashed curves: measured in the full nonlinear simulator.
